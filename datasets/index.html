Google Trends dataset: email your list

One dataset built to your list: up to 50 search terms, up to 5 countries, one timeframe. To start, email ops@tallyrake.com with three lines: terms, geos (two-letter country codes) and one timeframe, or leave the timeframe out for the default.

We read your list and write back the exact job. The price for a confirmed job is $149. We may decline a list.

Files by email: two CSVs, one XLSX workbook with a sheet for each, and SUMMARY.md. The 48 hours start when your payment clears. The files are due by email by the end of those 48 hours.

Timeframe: one window per job, named as now 7-d (hourly), today 1-m (daily), today 3-m (daily), today 12-m (weekly, the default), today 5-y (weekly), or two dates such as 2025-01-01 2025-12-31 (Google's own grain for that span). Every row carries the term, the country and the time it was fetched. Only interest-over-time rows have a timeframe column. Related-query rows have no timeframe column: the window is the one in the job, and their link column is a source link to Google Trends.

Scale: interest over time is scored 0 to 100 separately for each term in each country, where 100 is that term's peak in that country in the window. It is relative to itself, not a search count, and not comparable between terms or between countries. Each term is requested on its own. Related queries are not on that index: top queries carry Google's value scaled for that term in that country, and rising queries show growth as a percentage or Breakout. Neither is comparable between terms or between countries.

If Google does not return a term and country pair, SUMMARY.md lists the missing pairs, and if you ask we rerun those pairs once, at no charge.

Geos are GB, not UK. Worldwide is not offered. Tallyrake is not affiliated with Google.

A single-use payment link is sent only after you reply yes to the written job. No payment, no job. There is no buy button on this page.

Bigger or different jobs: by request

Other data types, more rows, or recurring refreshes are handled as requests. A quote is not a yes. We may decline a request, and no price or delivery time binds either side until we confirm the job in writing. A payment made before that written confirmation creates no job.
